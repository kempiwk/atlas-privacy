Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Atlas is a personal fitness tracking app for iOS. Your data belongs to you. We do not sell, share, or monetise your personal information in any way.

Data we collect

Atlas only collects data you explicitly enter or approve:

  • Workout logs — exercises, sets, reps, weight, duration, RPE
  • Body measurements you log (weight, body fat, chest, etc.)
  • Apple Health data you choose to connect (steps, active calories, body weight, workouts)
  • App preferences (units, settings)
  • Your email address, when you sign in with Apple ID (used solely to identify your account; we do not send marketing emails)

Where data is stored

  • All data is stored locally on your device using a SQLite database. The database file is protected by iOS full-disk encryption and is only accessible while your device is unlocked.
  • If you sign in with Apple ID, your workouts, goals, measurements, and custom exercises are also backed up to Supabase (our cloud database) so you can restore your data on a new device. Supabase stores data in EU data centres and does not use your data for advertising.
  • When you sign in with Apple ID, Apple provides us with your account identifier and email address (or Apple's private relay address if you choose Hide My Email). We do not receive your name.
  • We do not store data on any third-party advertising or analytics platforms

Apple Health

  • Atlas reads Health data only to display it within the app
  • Atlas writes workouts to Apple Health only when you enable this in Settings
  • Health data is never uploaded to any server
  • You can revoke Health access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. The only external service used is Supabase (cloud backup, optional) which stores data in an EU data centre under strict access controls.

Data deletion

To delete all local data: delete the Atlas app from your device.

To delete your cloud-backed data and account, use the "Delete Account" option in Profile. All data is deleted immediately. You can also email us to request deletion: support.kemp.labs@gmail.com

Children

Atlas is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
